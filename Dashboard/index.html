KPI definitions & interpretation

Churn rateChurned customers ÷ total customers × 100. A higher rate indicates greater customer loss.
Retention rateRetained customers ÷ total customers × 100. For this dataset, retention = 100% − churn rate.
Revenue at riskSum of monthly charges for customers marked Churn = Yes. This is estimated monthly recurring revenue exposure, not realized loss.
TenureAverage number of months customers have stayed. Shorter tenure can indicate early-life churn risk.

Dataset note: this supplied sample contains only 15 customer records. Treat results as a demonstration, not a statistically reliable forecast. Revenue at risk uses MonthlyCharges and excludes lifetime TotalCharges.
